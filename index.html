Common questions

How do I start a game?
  1. Tap the time control in the middle of the clock and pick one, or make your own.
  2. The player with Black taps their side to start White's clock, as in over-the-board play.
  3. After each move, tap your own half. Your clock stops and your opponent's starts.

A tap on the side that is waiting does nothing, so a slip onto your opponent's half can't cost you.

I pressed by mistake.

Tap Undo last press (the curved arrow in the middle). The mover's clock runs again from the moment of the press, and the seconds in between count for nobody.

How do I add or take away time, for a penalty?

Pause the game. Each clock then shows −1m, −10s, +10s and +1m. Resume when the times are right.

Which time controls are there?

Sudden death, Fischer increment, US delay and Bronstein delay, several stages (such as 90 minutes for 40 moves, then 30), byo-yomi and Canadian overtime for Go, hourglass, Armageddon, and Scrabble-style overtime. Custom lets you give each player a different time.

The clock paused when I left the app.

That is Pause when you leave in Settings › During a game: leaving the app or locking the phone pauses the running clock, as an arbiter would. Turn it off if you want the clock to keep running, for example to follow it on the Lock Screen. The screen never dims while Kingside is open.

How do I see the clock on the Lock Screen?

Turn on Lock Screen clock in Settings › Layout. Both clocks then show on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island while a game is on. With Pause when you leave on, locking the phone pauses the game, so the Lock Screen shows it paused.

Where are my games?

When you reset a game that has moves, Kingside asks how it ended. Pick a result to save the game in your history, with every move's think time. Open History from the clock or from Settings; tap a game for its summary, long-press it to delete it. History stays on your device.

How does the board game timer work?

Open it from the clock or from Settings. Choose 2 to 8 players and a time bank, a limit per turn, or a count-up. Tap a player to start, then tap the player whose turn it is to pass to the next one. Long-press a player to hand the turn to them out of order.

How do I change the language?

Kingside follows your device's language: English, German, Spanish, French, Portuguese, Russian, Japanese and Chinese. To use another one in Kingside only, go to the iPhone's Settings › Kingside › Language.

Is Kingside free?

Yes, every feature, with no ads and no subscription. If you enjoy it, you can leave an optional tip in Settings. Tips are handled by Apple; refunds are at reportaproblem.apple.com.
