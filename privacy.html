Kingside

Privacy Policy

Effective September 30, 2026 · Applies to Kingside: Chess Clock for iPhone and iPad

Kingside collects no data. There is no account, no server of ours, no advertising and no analytics. The only connections it makes are to Apple's App Store, for the optional tips and Apple's own rating prompt. Your games stay on your device.

What stays on your device

Your settings, the player names you type, your own time controls and the games you save to your history (results, dates and each move's think time) are stored inside the app on your iPhone or iPad. We never receive any of it. If you delete Kingside, this data is deleted with it.

Permissions

None. Kingside does not ask for the microphone, camera, photos, location, contacts or any other permission. Spoken time warnings use the iPhone's built-in speech, on the device.

Lock Screen and Dynamic Island

With Lock Screen clock on, the app shows the two clocks, the player names and the time control as a Live Activity on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. It is updated by the app on your device; no push service or server is involved. Anyone who can see your Lock Screen can see it, so leave names empty or turn the setting off if that matters to you.

Tips

Tips are optional in-app purchases processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. To show their prices and to finish a tip, the app connects to the App Store. Kingside never sees your payment details or Apple ID. Apple gives developers only anonymous sales totals.

Ratings

After a few finished games, Kingside may ask iOS to show Apple's standard "Enjoying Kingside?" rating prompt. iOS decides whether it appears, and any rating or review goes to Apple under Apple's terms. Kingside never learns whether you rated it or what you wrote.

Children

Kingside collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If Kingside ever changes how it handles data, this page will say so before the change reaches the App Store, with a new effective date. Questions: rh418899@gmail.com.
